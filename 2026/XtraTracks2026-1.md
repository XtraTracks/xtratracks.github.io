< ## <a href="https://www.uniklinikum-jena.de/imsid/%C3%9Cber+uns/Kontaktpersonen.html">Prof. Dr. Cord Spreckelsen</a> - Institute of Medical Statistics, Computer and Data Sciences - Jena University Hospital 
<img src="/images/2026/Spreckelsen_Cord.jpg?raw=true" height="200" />

<p style="font-size:11px">Cord Spreckelsen is Professor of Medical Informatics at the Institute for Medical Statistics, Informatics and Data Sciences at Jena University Hospital. Prior to this, he worked for many years as a senior scientist at the Institute for Medical Informatics at RWTH Aachen University. From 2009 to 2025, he was head of the working group ‘Decision Support in Healthcare’ (formerly ‘Knowledge-Based Systems’) of the German Society for Medical Informatics, Biometrics and Epidemiology (GMDS).

His research team has many years of experience in applying both logic-based and data-driven AI methods in a medical context. These applications include AI-supported medical decision support, the imputation and prediction of medical time series using generative AI, and AI-supported optimisation of hospital logistics. A current research project deals with the classification and prediction of long/post-COVID from longer-term symptom progression data. Cord Spreckelsen's working group contributed to the development of federated data management approaches and cross-location, privacy-friendly deep learning. Based on generative AI approaches, the team also developed methods for anonymising medical images and DNA sequence data through data synthesis with algorithmic assessment of the re-identification risk.</p>
